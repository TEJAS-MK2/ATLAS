# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in ATLAS a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainers through a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Reports will be reviewed as fairly and promptly as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces. Maintainers will consider the nature and impact of the behaviour.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
